# Darkness Expansion
The Darkness Invades!

A mod with new content including an elite type, shrine, artifact, and items.

All content is implemented through custom mechanics.
## Shrines
### Darkness Potential
Three darkness potentials will always spawn when a darkness shrine spawns. Interacting with them allows you to sacrifice an item to the darkness.
### Shrine of Darkness
After one item has been sacrificed to a darkness potential, the shrine of darkness may be activated to summon a dark elite type boss. This boss gains all of the items that were sacrificed to the shrine. Defeating the boss will cause the shrine to drop upgraded versions of all of the items sacrificed:

White -> Green -> Yellow -> Red -> 5 Whites
## Darkness Elite
### Darkness Level
Enemies have a chance to spawn as a darkness elite, depending on the darkness level. The darkness level is increased by the following: 

Activating a Shrine of Darkness

Holding a Dark Item

Having the Artifact of Darkness Enabled
### Elite Features
Darkness elites will have increased health and damage, based on the darkness level. They will also gain a random dark item.
## Artifact of Darkness
The artifact of darkness increases the darkness level by 1 as well as starts the players with a random dark item.
## Darkness Items
### Creating a Darkness Items
Darkness items can be gained through the following sources:

Starting with the artifact of darkness

Offering each Darkness Potential the same yellow item will yield a dark item drop instead of 3 reds.
